## Why this source matters
- This bundle was assembled to replace weak promotional differentiation language with stronger, defensible scientific wording.

## Source excerpt or extracted evidence
- Estriol can be described as a weak or short-acting estrogen with lower receptor affinity or weaker transactivation than estradiol in mechanistic literature.
- Vaginal-use studies and reviews support a reassuring but not zero systemic hormone effect profile.
- The evidence does not support strong absolute wording such as end-product metabolite, no conversion back to estradiol or estrone, or a clinically proven nuclear-retention advantage in this slide context.
- A careful comparison theme against conjugated equine estrogens may be possible at the systemic-hormone-effect level, but not as a broad superiority claim.
