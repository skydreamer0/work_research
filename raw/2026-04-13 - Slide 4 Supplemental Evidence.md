## Why this source matters
- This bundle was assembled to determine whether Slide 4 can be strengthened beyond a vague tissue-effect statement.

## Source excerpt or extracted evidence
- Estriol-specific studies support epithelial maturation, cytology shift, and restoration of atrophic vaginal mucosal findings.
- Vaginal maturation index improvement is supported most directly by estriol-containing or local estrogen studies, but some support is class-level rather than estriol-only.
- Stronger claims about epithelial thickness and collagen/connective tissue support are better supported at the local vaginal estrogen class level than by estriol-specific human biopsy data.
- No sufficiently strong estriol-specific human source was identified to support a clean vascularization claim.
