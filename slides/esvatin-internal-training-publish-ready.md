Title: Understanding GSM

Slide 5
Title: Evidence of Cytology Improvement
Subtitle: Vaginal cytology shifts toward a healthier epithelial pattern
Body bullets:
- Intravaginal estrogen improved vaginal cytology in pooled evidence.
- Superficial cells increased by 19.28%.
- Parabasal cells decreased by 24.85%.
- These findings support reversal of an atrophic epithelial pattern.
Presenter notes:
This is one of the strongest evidence slides in the deck because the numeric support is direct. The key point is that local estrogen therapy is not only about how a patient feels. There is also measurable epithelial change. These are pooled estimates, so they should be presented as meta-analytic evidence rather than as a product-specific effect.
