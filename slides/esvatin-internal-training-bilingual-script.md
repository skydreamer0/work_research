# Esvatin Internal Training Bilingual Script

## Slide 1
EN: We will begin with disease understanding, treatment logic, and responsible communication rather than promotion. One product fact we can state clearly is that Esvatin is an estriol 0.5 milligram vaginal tablet.
ZH: 我們會先從疾病理解、治療邏輯與負責任的溝通開始，而不是直接進入推廣。現在可以明確講的產品事實是，Esvatin 是 `estriol 0.5 mg` 的陰道錠。

## Slide 2
EN: GSM is linked to estrogen decline after menopause and can affect several genitourinary tissues, including the vulva, vagina, urethra, and bladder.
ZH: GSM 和停經後雌激素下降有關，可能影響多個泌尿生殖組織，包括外陰、陰道、尿道與膀胱。

## Slide 3
EN: GSM is not just a tissue change. It can affect comfort, intimacy, and day-to-day quality of life, and patients may not always bring these symptoms up on their own.
ZH: GSM 不只是組織變化，它也會影響舒適度、親密關係與日常生活品質，而且病人不一定會主動提起這些症狀。

## Slide 4
EN: The available evidence supports local tissue-level estrogenic activity, epithelial maturation, and restoration of atrophic mucosal findings.
ZH: 目前可用的證據支持局部組織層次的雌激素作用、上皮成熟，以及萎縮性黏膜表現的改善。

## Slide 5
EN: Pooled evidence shows improved vaginal cytology, with superficial cells increasing and parabasal cells decreasing.
ZH: 綜合證據顯示陰道細胞學有改善，表層細胞增加，而基底旁細胞下降。

## Slide 6
EN: The evidence also supports a reduction in vaginal pH and an improvement in dyspareunia, linking tissue change with symptom benefit.
ZH: 證據也支持陰道 pH 降低與性交疼痛改善，顯示組織變化與症狀改善之間有連結。

## Slide 7
EN: The safer message is that reviewed studies did not identify clinically relevant long-term serum hormone changes, while minimal transient gonadotropin changes were reported in some studies.
ZH: 比較安全的說法是，回顧文獻沒有發現具臨床意義的長期血清荷爾蒙變化，但部分研究有提到輕微且暫時性的促性腺激素變化。

## Slide 8
EN: Estriol can be described as a weak or short-acting estrogen, and vaginal-use evidence supports limited systemic hormonal stimulation rather than strong systemic effects.
ZH: Estriol 可以被描述為較弱或作用較短的雌激素，而陰道使用的證據支持的是有限的全身性荷爾蒙刺激，而不是明顯的全身性作用。

## Slide 9
EN: The evidence does not prove that one dosage form is clinically more effective than another, but it does support discussion of convenience, preference, and persistence across formats.
ZH: 目前證據沒有證明某一種劑型在臨床效果上一定更好，但可以支持我們討論不同劑型在便利性、偏好與持續使用上的差異。

## Slide 10
EN: The key takeaway is to keep product positioning limited to what we can verify for Esvatin itself, while using class-level evidence carefully and accurately.
ZH: 最重要的結論是，產品定位要限於我們能為 Esvatin 本身驗證的內容，同時對於 class-level 的證據要謹慎且準確地使用。
