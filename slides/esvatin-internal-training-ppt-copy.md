# Esvatin Internal Training PPT Copy

## Slide 1
Title: Esvatin: Local Estrogen Therapy for Genitourinary Health
Subtitle: Understanding GSM and its treatment logic
Bullets:
- Esvatin is an estriol 0.5 mg vaginal tablet
- Understand GSM before discussing therapy
- Keep communication evidence-linked

## Slide 2
Title: Understanding GSM
Subtitle: A common postmenopausal condition linked to estrogen decline
Bullets:
- Common in postmenopausal women
- Driven mainly by estrogen decline
- May affect the vulva, vagina, urethra, and bladder

## Slide 3
Title: Why GSM Matters Clinically
Subtitle: Symptoms can affect comfort, intimacy, and quality of life
Bullets:
- Dryness, irritation, dyspareunia, urinary discomfort
- Can affect comfort and quality of life
- Patients may not raise symptoms proactively

## Slide 4
Title: Mechanism and Tissue-Level Effects
Subtitle: Local tissue response is the key concept
Bullets:
- Vaginal estriol supports local estrogenic activity
- Evidence supports epithelial maturation
- Evidence supports restoration of atrophic mucosal findings
- Avoid overstating collagen or vascular claims

## Slide 5
Title: Evidence of Cytology Improvement
Subtitle: Vaginal cytology shifts toward a healthier epithelial pattern
Bullets:
- Intravaginal estrogen improved vaginal cytology
- Superficial cells: +19.28%
- Parabasal cells: -24.85%
- Pooled evidence, not product-specific proof

## Slide 6
Title: pH Normalization and Symptom Relief
Subtitle: Tissue improvement can be linked to symptom improvement
Bullets:
- Vaginal pH: -0.94
- Dyspareunia: -0.52
- 95% CI: -0.63 to -0.41
- Supports symptom improvement with local therapy

## Slide 7
Title: Local Use and Reassuring Hormonal Findings
Subtitle: Limited clinically relevant long-term hormone change
Bullets:
- No clinically relevant long-term serum hormone changes in review
- Reassuring local-use safety discussion
- Minimal transient gonadotropin changes reported
- Avoid stronger wording unless separately verified

## Slide 8
Title: Estriol in the Context of Local Therapy
Subtitle: A careful scientific distinction
Bullets:
- Estriol may be described as weak or short-acting
- Differentiation is best kept conceptual
- Vaginal-use studies support limited systemic hormonal stimulation
- Avoid absolute metabolic claims

## Slide 9
Title: Practical Use and Patient Acceptance
Subtitle: Formulation matters for experience and persistence
Bullets:
- Dosage form can influence convenience and acceptance
- Some evidence supports preference differences across formats
- Practical-use factors may influence persistence
- Do not equate preference with efficacy superiority

## Slide 10
Title: Key Messages for Practice
Subtitle: Four take-home points
Bullets:
- GSM is clinically meaningful
- Local estriol evidence supports symptom and cytology improvement
- Reviewed evidence is reassuring when wording stays accurate
- Keep Esvatin claims limited to verified product facts
