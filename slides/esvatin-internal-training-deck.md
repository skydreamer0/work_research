Slide 5
Title: Evidence of Cytology Improvement
Subtitle: Vaginal cytology shifts toward a healthier epithelial pattern
Body bullets:
- Intravaginal estrogen improved vaginal cytology in pooled evidence. (`raw/2026-04-13 - Ali 2024 - evidence.md`)
- Superficial cells increased by 19.28%. (`raw/2026-04-13 - Ali 2024 - evidence.md`)
- Parabasal cells decreased by 24.85%. (`raw/2026-04-13 - Ali 2024 - evidence.md`)
- These changes support reversal of an atrophic epithelial pattern. (`raw/2026-04-13 - Ali 2024 - evidence.md`)
Presenter notes:
This is one of the strongest evidence slides in the deck because the source directly supports the numbers. In the meta-analysis, intravaginal estrogen was associated with more superficial cells and fewer parabasal cells, which is consistent with a healthier vaginal epithelial pattern. The key teaching point is that local therapy is not only about how the patient feels; there is also measurable epithelial change. These are pooled estimates, so the safest phrasing is to present them as meta-analytic evidence rather than as a product-specific effect.
