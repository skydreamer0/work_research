## Decision Rules

- `partial`: claim needs conservative wording or extra review.
- `blocked`: claim cannot enter the draft.

## Draft Guidance
- Keep only verified claims in the slide body.
- Move borderline statements to internal notes or review.
- For external-candidate output, use the most conservative supported wording.
