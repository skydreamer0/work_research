# Esvatin Safe Wording Matrix

## Purpose
This matrix translates the current evidence base into release decisions for slide writing.

Use this file before finalizing any deck for circulation, review, or presentation.

## Classes
- `Use now`: supported strongly enough for the current intended context
- `Use with caution`: support exists, but wording must stay narrow
- `Do not use`: not adequately supported or too risky in the current source set

## Product Identity

### Use now
- Esvatin is an estriol 0.5 mg vaginal tablet.

### Use with caution
- Esvatin is discussed in this deck within a broader estriol or local-estrogen evidence context.

### Do not use
- Esvatin is label-supported for GSM.
- Esvatin has product-specific proof of tissue remodeling, low systemic absorption, or superior convenience.

## Disease Framing

### Use now
- GSM is a common postmenopausal condition.
- GSM is associated with estrogen decline.
- GSM can involve the vulva, vagina, urethra, and bladder.
- GSM symptoms can affect comfort, intimacy, and quality of life.

### Use with caution
- Symptoms may persist or worsen over time.
- Patients may not proactively report symptoms.

### Do not use
- Any specific prevalence number or disease-burden statistic not directly sourced in this workspace.

## Tissue-Level Effects

### Use now
- Vaginal estriol supports local tissue-level estrogenic activity.
- Supplemental evidence supports epithelial maturation.
- Supplemental evidence supports restoration of atrophic mucosal findings.
- Vaginal maturation shift can be discussed conservatively.

### Use with caution
- Tissue support can be discussed beyond symptom relief alone.
- Epithelial thickness or connective tissue support may be mentioned only as local-estrogen class-level context, not as a clean estriol-only fact.

### Do not use
- Vascularization improvement.
- Detailed collagen remodeling claims as if directly established for Esvatin.

## Cytology And Symptom Outcomes

### Use now
- Intravaginal estrogen improved vaginal cytology in pooled evidence.
- Superficial cells increased by 19.28%.
- Parabasal cells decreased by 24.85%.
- Vaginal pH changed by -0.94.
- Dyspareunia changed by -0.52 with a 95% CI of -0.63 to -0.41.

### Use with caution
- These findings support reversal of an atrophic epithelial pattern.
- These findings support restoration toward a healthier vaginal environment.

### Do not use
- Any wording that turns pooled intravaginal estrogen evidence into direct Esvatin-specific efficacy.

## Safety And Systemic Hormone Effects

### Use now
- A systematic review found no clinically relevant long-term serum hormone changes with vaginal estriol.
- Minimal transient gonadotropin changes were reported in some studies.
- Vaginal-use studies support local action with limited, but not zero, systemic hormonal stimulation.

### Use with caution
- Local use can be discussed as reassuring when wording stays evidence-based.
- Limited systemic stimulation is safer than minimal absorption.

### Do not use
- Serum estradiol remains within the postmenopausal range.
- Zero systemic exposure.
- Esvatin-specific low systemic absorption.

## Estriol Differentiation

### Use now
- Estriol can be described as a weak or short-acting estrogen in mechanistic literature.
- Differentiation is easier to support at the receptor and systemic-hormone level than with broad metabolic claims.

### Use with caution
- Vaginal estriol has not been shown to cause clinically relevant long-term increases in serum estrone or estradiol in reviewed studies.
- Comparative framing versus other estrogen classes should stay conceptual and conservative.

### Do not use
- End-product metabolite.
- Not converted back to estradiol or estrone as an absolute statement.
- Nuclear-retention advantage.
- Broad superiority versus conjugated estrogens.

## Formulation And Practical Use

### Use now
- Dosage form can influence convenience, acceptance, and persistence in local estrogen therapy.
- Some comparative and real-world evidence supports preference or persistence differences across formats.

### Use with caution
- Practical-use factors may influence treatment persistence.
- Formulation preference can be discussed in training as a patient-experience topic.

### Do not use
- Formulation superiority equals efficacy superiority.
- Esvatin-specific convenience or persistence advantage without product-specific data.

### Conservative external-candidate release
- Suitable only as a review candidate.
- Must keep product claims limited to verified identity facts unless stronger product-specific documentation is added.
