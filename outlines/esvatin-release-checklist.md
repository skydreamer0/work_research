# Esvatin Release Checklist

## Purpose
Use this checklist before circulating the deck outside the working team.

## Evidence Checks
- Every numerical claim maps to a file in `raw/`.
- Every product statement is checked against `raw/2026-04-13 - Esvatin Product-Specific Evidence.md`.
- No pooled estriol or local-estrogen evidence is presented as Esvatin-specific proof.
- All higher-risk claims have been removed or softened according to `outlines/esvatin-safe-wording-matrix.md`.

## Slide Checks
- Slide 4 avoids vascularization and hard collagen-remodeling language.
- Slide 7 avoids the phrase `serum estradiol remains within the postmenopausal range`.
- Slide 8 avoids end-product metabolite and nuclear-retention wording.
- Slide 9 avoids efficacy superiority and product-specific convenience claims.

## Audience Checks
- Internal deck is used for training, not as a field leave-behind.
- External-candidate deck is sent for review before any broader use.
- Presenter understands which statements are class-level and which are product-specific.

## Final Readiness
- Language is oral, concise, and non-exaggerated.
- Visual suggestions match the actual claims shown on the slide.
- Reviewer sign-off is obtained if the deck will be used beyond internal training.
